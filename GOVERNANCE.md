# Governance

11 October is early-stage. This guide describes responsibilities, not ranks or formal membership.

## Responsibilities

- **Contributor:** makes or reviews a useful change.
- **Maintainer:** cares for a repository, reviews contributions, and keeps project work safe and understandable.
- **Problem Lead:** coordinates investigation and decisions for an accepted Problem ID.
- **Reviewer:** checks technical reasoning, evidence, safety, or reproducibility.
- **Article Contributor:** helps document the investigation and results.
- **Requester:** identifies the original problem or provides context; this does not create an obligation to join or approve a solution.
- **Tester:** reproduces a procedure or evaluates a prototype and records conditions and results.

One person may hold more than one responsibility. Contributors do not need an organization title to do useful work.

## Decisions

Maintainers are responsible for accepting, declining, or archiving problem reports and for merging repository changes. Decisions should be made in the relevant issue or pull request when public discussion is appropriate, with a short reason and links to the evidence considered.

An accepted problem should have a named Problem Lead before substantial work begins. The lead keeps the source, scope, risks, and validation plan visible. Contributors should be consulted on decisions that materially change work they own.

When maintainers disagree, they should first try to resolve the question with evidence and the project’s stated requirements. A maintainer with a conflict of interest should disclose it and step back from the affected decision where practical.

## Scope and safety

Acceptance is not a promise to deliver. Maintainers may narrow, pause, decline, or stop work when the evidence, capability, safety, privacy, licensing, or validation path is inadequate. Explain the decision without exposing private information.
