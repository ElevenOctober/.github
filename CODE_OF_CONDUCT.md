# Code of Conduct

This community aims to make practical engineering collaboration respectful, safe, and useful to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

This is a local adaptation informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected conduct

Participants should communicate respectfully, consider the effect of their words and actions, give technical feedback about the work rather than a person’s worth, and make room for different experience levels.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone’s private information without permission, deliberate disruption, and pressuring others to ignore safety or privacy concerns.

## Responding to concerns

Maintainers will review reports privately, gather relevant context, and choose a proportionate response. Possible responses include a request to stop, removal of harmful content, a temporary participation limit, or removal from a project space. A person affected by a decision may ask for a review by a maintainer who was not directly involved, where one is available.

Reports and identifying details should be shared only with people who need them to handle the matter. No one should retaliate against a person who raises a concern in good faith.

## Reporting

Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue. No dedicated private reporting address is currently published. Contact an organization maintainer through a private channel you already trust and ask for a safe reporting route without posting incident details publicly. If the concern involves a platform policy violation, use GitHub’s reporting tools.

Organization maintainers should establish and publish a monitored private reporting route before expanding community recruitment.
